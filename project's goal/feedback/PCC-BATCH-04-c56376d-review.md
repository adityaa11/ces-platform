## Advisory observations

- The repository membership join and browser-safe result shape are directionally aligned with the authorization and sensitive-data boundaries, but they do not cure the page-to-repository identity/transport defects above.
- Full responsive/theme/accessibility validation of the production card is intentionally deferred to PCC-005, which explicitly owns the client dialog, shared presentation refactor, and visual validation. It is not a PCC-004 finding.
- The ticket records three unrelated pre-existing application lint violations in `RuntimeFixtureRoute.tsx` and `vite.config.ts`; they are outside the PCC-004 changed boundary and are not independently treated as a PCC-004 finding.
- The Worker/Cloudflare runtime is not treated as mandatory scope: the frozen PCC-004 ticket names Docker Compose for validation but does not identify a production deployment runtime.

## Decision

PCC-BATCH-04 cannot receive `PASS`. The authenticated browser observation confirms that sign-in and the empty `/home` state work. Three implementation-repairable PCC-004 findings remain: the page does not pass the resolved user ID directly into the read service, the read path forwards cookies to a request-Host-derived destination, and the projection does not validate all persisted conditions behind `Waiting for extraction`. The result is `CHANGES_REQUIRED`; keep PCC-004 at `awaiting_review`. CFC may address only CK-001 through CK-003. PCC-005 remains gated on a later PCC-004 `PASS` and owns the full card UI/visual validation work described by its ticket.
