# CES-GF-DAPE-014 — Policy Gap Analysis and Standards Research

**Priority:** P3 — Governed policy evolution  
**Status:** Planned

## Goal

Inspect existing policy semantics before proposing the smallest justified
registry change, with authoritative standards research when needed.

## Work

- Add policy-coverage analyzer and semantic existing-policy search.
- Distinguish reuse, trigger extension, policy revision, new policy, capability
  gap, and no-policy-required outcomes.
- Register a provider-neutral standards-research agent restricted to approved
  authoritative sources and version-qualified citations.
- Produce evidence-bounded change proposals without compliance claims.
- Require human review; research agents cannot publish registry content.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Existing policy semantics are inspected before proposing additions.
- [ ] Research sources are authoritative, dated/versioned, and traceable.
- [ ] Unsupported claims and unverifiable citations fail review.
- [ ] Proposals cannot weaken active obligations automatically.
- [ ] Normal CI uses deterministic research fixtures.
