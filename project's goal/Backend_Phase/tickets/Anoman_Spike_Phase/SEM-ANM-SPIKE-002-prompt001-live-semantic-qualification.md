## Provider response handling and validation

The only permitted output normalization is removal of one optional, matching outer Markdown JSON fence (` ```json` or the allowed plain opening fence, followed by its matching closing fence) when it wraps the entire response. ### Oracle comparison tolerance

The oracle tests preservation of business meaning, not byte-equal free text. A bounded, deterministic normalization may be used only for oracle comparison; it may trim, lowercase, collapse whitespace, remove harmless surrounding punctuation, remove a leading `a`/`an`/`the`, and use a small source-anchored inflection comparison where appropriate. It must never mutate stored provider output, Zod input, or finalized Atlas output, and must not alter semantic kind, enum meaning, resolution, numbers, limit direction, identity, applicability/temporal distinction, scope, or proposition presence.

Accept harmless S1 variation such as `customer`/`the customer`, `order`/`an order`/`the order`, and `submit`/`submits`/`submission`; accept S4 temporal wording `before processing` or `approval occurs before processing`. Reject material changes including customer to supervisor, order to invoice, submit to approve, possible to permitted/required, maximum 2 to 3, per-order to per-customer, missing applicability turned into an invented trigger, or temporal ordering represented as applicability.

Required S1-S4 negative oracle cases include:

- S1 requests clarification solely because submission method is missing.
- S2 loses 2, maximum direction, product, per-order scope, or constraint meaning.
- S3 becomes a business proposition.
- S4 becomes `required` or `permitted`, treats `before processing` as an applicability condition, resolves without support, omits clarification, invents a trigger/threshold/approver, or otherwise collapses temporal order into applicability.
