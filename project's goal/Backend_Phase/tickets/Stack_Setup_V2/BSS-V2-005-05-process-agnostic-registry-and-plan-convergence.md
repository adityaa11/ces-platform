## Outcome

Make the **existing** capacity catalogue, `planDesiredAdmissionProfile`, and immutable plan-publication seam extensible to any **server-registered process kind**, rather than a fixed TypeScript enum requiring edits to the capacity algorithm for new process types. The registry selects capability, qualified route/quota domain, service class, fairness scope, versioned workload/request estimator/output reservation, configured protection/weights/caps, eligibility and activation policy. Admission resources are still independently measured as RPM/TPM/RPD/concurrency. The registry must not grant capabilities, credentials, source access, model-quality acceptance or provider quotas on the basis of an arbitrary name.

Versioned, **already-authorized and qualified** process/workload/policy changes or BSS-V2-005 provider-capacity-profile revisions affecting an eligible quota domain cause **durable deduplicated recalculation, validation and immutable publication/activation of a new `DesiredAdmissionProfile` version**, followed by the **existing** BSS-V2-006 plan-activation wakeup. Detecting an input version is not itself authority to activate a route, process, provider or model. Repeated identical effective input/configuration must not republish or advance the activation revision; qualified changed inputs must use the existing per-domain atomic version/activation fence, with stale/concurrent updates rejected or reconciled without lost changes. Do not update prior versions or overwrite in-flight reservation references. Unknown/unqualified process, inactive workload, route, quota source or estimator **fails closed**; no automatic provider/model activation merely because a process was registered.

## Process-neutral versus demand-driven responsibilities

- **BSS-V2-005:** compute policy and advertised resource ceiling from the **validated active process/profile set**, under explicit operator-controlled reserve/weights/safety rules; a new registration/configuration version may change desired allocation automatically. Its deterministic planner must **not** use transient queue depth, remaining TPM or runtime usage as planning input.
- **BSS-V2-006:** run-time actual work/attempt admission, per-domain pressure/cooldown, fair borrowing among backlogged eligible lanes, and one reservation per outbound attempt. Unused background allocation may be borrowed as permitted; **hard interactive reserve is never lent to background**. New queue activity can shift who gets admitted without constantly regenerating or rewriting the DesiredAdmissionProfile.
- **Consumers (including IDSER planner):** ask for an authenticated bounded budget/constraint view through versioned provider-neutral interfaces; they may size a request but not publish a capacity plan or reserve/dispatch provider work.
- **Policy:** dynamic adaptation is bounded by configured source-of-truth limits and explicit protection. No fabricated provider limits, implicit equal fairness, unbounded adaptive priority, or learned weights without approved policy.
