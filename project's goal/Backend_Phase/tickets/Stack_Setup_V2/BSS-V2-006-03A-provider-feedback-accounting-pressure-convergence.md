## State transitions and transactional rules

### A. Identity and atomic admission

- Work/attempt: `waiting -> reserved -> dispatched/transport-may-have-started -> terminal -> reconciled`, retaining BSS-V2-006-01 fences and `006-02` single-transport authority. A canceled/expired definitely-untransmitted attempt can terminalize with full eligible release. `dispatched` alone does not prove transport happened, but **uncertainty may not be downgraded** by a later adapter result.
- Inside the **existing BSS-V2-006-02 authoritative admission transaction/serialization** (the background and targeted-interactive entrypoints in migration `0036`): resolve active plan, exact current windows, configured route/domain and pressure; evaluate per-dimension available+held+deficit state with protected reserve; retain approved fairness selection; create exactly one matching reservation and one job on `bridge-provider-admitted-execution-v1`. Extend/refactor that common admission authority **in place**; never clone it into a `03A`-only admission path or replace approved behavior with a test-specific implementation. No between-check/send bypass and no new route selection for an existing attempt.
- Reservations and feedback retain immutable attribution: attempt, reservation, chosen route, canonical domain, active plan/capacity/window-policy/accounting versions, dimension/window identities and fence. Waiting work may be revalidated/rebased on plan activation; **in-flight** work retains original authorization. The internal worker must atomically claim the already-persisted job/dispatch/reservation **before** calling the adapter transport. A crash or lost acknowledgment **after a claim** leaves a potentially transmitted attempt; replay cannot send it again. Crash **before claim** may process the original queued job only after rechecking the same selected-route/plan/deadline fence. The claim primitive may be renamed from `claim_fake_provider_transport` to production-neutral wording without changing predecessor at-most-once behavior. New plans cannot recode prior consumption as free capacity.

### B. Operational feedback ingress

Accept only a bounded, typed, provider-neutral event after adapter-local interpretation of documented vendor semantics: `feedbackId`, `reservationId`, `attemptId`, fenced transport disposition, selected-route/qualification identity, canonical domain, authorizing plan/window-policy revision, provider observation source/trust classification and freshness, and **optional** per-dimension trustworthy quota consumption, 429/status, `Retry-After` and qualified lower-limit/remaining/reset observations. Exact field naming may follow existing implementation conventions, but these identities and validations are mandatory.

A missing field stays **unknown**, never zero; timestamps, windows, dimensions, nonnegative bounded units, replay identity and observation freshness are validated before operational mutation. Wrong-route/domain/plan, unrecognized headers, invalid negative/overflow values, stale/conflicting replay and untrusted source cannot produce numeric authority or lift pressure. Persist only minimized redacted operational facts; raw headers, HTTP bodies, prompts, documents, credentials, vendor tokens and model response text remain outside operational tables.

Adapter status `429` alone is not evidence of RPM, TPM, RPD, a reset instant, a shared account or a new permanent provider limit. Provider SDK automatic retries must be disabled for admitted transport; every retry/probe needs a **new** BSS work attempt and reservation.

### C. Exact accounting and safe release

Maintain domain-owned operational accounting by the **actual dimension and versioned window identity** `(quotaDomainId, capacity/window-policy version, dimension, window instance)`, plus instantaneous in-flight concurrency. A single global `heldResourceEnvelope` cannot release all dimensions based on any unrelated expired window.

- Definitely not transmitted, verified against BSS's own transport fence: release the eligible reservation once; no request/day/token spend and no transport. An untrusted adapter's "not sent" cannot override a durable `may_have_started` fence.
- Possibly transmitted with unknown usage: count request/RPM and RPD once, retain reserved TPM (or any unknown metered dimension) in **its own** window and keep concurrency unavailable until a **durable proven transport-completion fence**. A timeout/connection loss/worker crash is not proof that the external operation ended: persist `transport_completion_unknown` and conservatively block or retain the relevant concurrency seat. A later trusted terminal adapter outcome can settle it; an explicit operator-authorized reconciliation decision may safely close the uncertainty only when it is accompanied by evidence of completion or termination. Without such evidence, remain blocked (do not infer release from elapsed time or a new plan). No assumption of an always-available vendor status API.
- Trustworthy actual consumption: after durable terminal observation, keep transmitted request/RPM and RPD as spent according to the approved accounting policy, replace reserved refundable token usage with exact attributed actual TPM, and release concurrency. Under-use returns **only the difference in the same window**; over-use persists a nonnegative deficit/debit limiting future admission in that same domain/window. Known dimensions may reconcile while omitted dimensions remain unknown independently.
- A window's charge/hold/deficit expires or rolls forward **only** under its approved fixed/rolling/refill/provider-reset/daily policy with witnessed or deterministic safe boundary; unknown reset/refill never means midnight, immediate release, or unlimited capacity. For plan vN→vN+1 **within the same canonical domain**, old nonexpired window liabilities remain charged to the matching physical quota window across policy revisions: resolve compatible window identities under the approved window-policy semantics, and do not reset consumption merely because `desiredProfileVersion` changed. If window policies or quota ownership cannot be proven compatible, retain old charges conservatively and block uncertain future capacity until qualified evidence establishes a safe boundary; do not merge distinct policies into an optimistic new window. Protected interactive reserve is a policy portion of the same domain vector, not an extra ledger. Restart, duplicate terminal result, late feedback and old-plan overlap cannot double-refund, double-spend or release another window.

Uniqueness is enforced both by **reservation terminal-reconcile identity** and feedback event identity: byte-/canonically identical duplicate outcome returns idempotent result; same event ID with different payload or second terminal outcome for the same reservation rejects or remains explicitly nonmutating under a specified bounded conflict rule. No second event may mutate already-final consumption without an independently authorized correction protocol (not in `03A`).

### D. Scoped runtime pressure and bounded recovery

Operational pressure is durable and monotonically conservative within its authorization scope. For each dimension/window, effective numerical ceiling is `min(active BSS-V2-005 authorized ceiling, trustworthy lower domain-scoped observed ceiling)`. A documented observation may constrain a **route** operationally; it must not manufacture a separate route numeric account. Proven shared quota-domain pressure constrains every route sharing that account; independent domains are unaffected.

- **Frozen policy authority:** introduce a Bridge-owned, operator-configured immutable `RuntimePressureSafetyPolicy` reference containing `policyId`, `version`, domain/route applicability, `maximumAcceptedRetryAfter`, `metadataFreeCooldown`, `probeBudget=1`, explicit bounded `probeEligibilityDeadline` and conservative blocked disposition. The exact duration values are **configuration**, never guessed from provider messages or hard-coded as provider facts; no configured qualified values means **no probe and fail-closed blocked outcome**. The policy belongs to 03A runtime operations, does not mutate BSS-V2-005 numeric limits, is activated only by existing trusted server/operator configuration, and changes by new version. For 429 with verified `Retry-After`, accept only positive, finite in-policy intervals; out-of-bounds/untrusted values become metadata-free incidents rather than unbounded waits. For 429 without trustworthy metadata, use `metadataFreeCooldown` when configured and persist a durable scope-specific incident; otherwise remain blocked until separately qualified evidence or policy.
- After the configured cooldown, permit **at most one** new explicitly reserved probe for the same stable incident/scope under a durable atomic claim and a configured absolute eligibility deadline; a replay, canceled probe, or lost acknowledgment cannot reset its budget. Probe failure/unknown outcome exhausts that incident's budget and leaves scoped admission conservatively blocked pending qualified new recovery evidence or an independently authorized new policy decision; it **never** creates another incident simply to retry recursively. Verified recovery only releases the matching eligible incident hold, never unrelated numeric clamps or window usage. Probe obeys actual quota/resource reservation, interactive deadlines and cancellation; it is not free transport or a hidden SDK retry.
- Store stable pressure incident identity keyed to verified origin/scope, observed-at/source, route/domain scope confidence, applicable safety-policy version, dimension/window when justified, bounded cooldown/probe deadline, recovery evidence and probe claim/spend. Duplicate, late, out-of-order, wrong-route or higher raw observations cannot lift pressure, mint a new incident to bypass the one-probe budget, or reset spent probe budget. Post-restart state is replay-safe; natural cooldown expiry makes a single policy-qualified probe *eligible*, not unconditionally safe for unrestricted admission. Safe window expiry applies only to its matching dimension/window.
- Never infer an infinite provider cooldown from a malformed timestamp while continuing silent retries. An unresolvable ambiguous event results in an explicit bounded conservative non-success/blocked decision and no transport.
