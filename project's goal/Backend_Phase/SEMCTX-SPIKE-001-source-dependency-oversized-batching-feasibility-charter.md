## 0.A. Original frozen reason (v0.1; retained without changing its meaning)

The newly recovered visual/table evidence establishes that Atlas currently has **no trusted inter-locator relationship** through which a visual title/legend may govern a table-owned semantic assertion. The current source packet enumerates whole-document text/table/labeled-visual units, has no reference-context channel and cannot interpret unlabeled verified image assets; extraction acceptance nevertheless requires complete accounting for normalized visual locators with `assetRef`. The existing reconciliation selector compares **semantic candidates**; it cannot prove missing source/table/image relationships after an ungrounded assertion has already been produced.

An additive human-attested sidecar was considered, but **a relationship graph can connect more evidence than one bounded provider request can carry**. A sidecar alone does not solve batching, and treating an entire connected corpus as one atomic request risks an unavoidable oversized batch. Conversely, arbitrarily splitting that corpus risks losing the qualifying condition, legend, merged header, negation, or scope that gives a statement its meaning.

**Unresolved planning question:** Can Atlas deterministically partition a connected source corpus into separately owned work units with *only explicitly authorized, meaning-essential reference context*, while preserving full evidence coverage and identifying cases that cannot safely fit? If not, what exact conditions must stop extraction or require a new human/structural trust contract?

**Why now:** This answer must precede freezing the `08-01-02` sidecar design and `08-02-01` packet ownership; otherwise the chosen relationship authority may be unusable by `IDSER-012-02-01` resource-bounded planning and force costly rework. This spike tests whether structure-aware local grouping, candidate reconciliation and a narrow provenance contract suffice; **it does not assume they do**.

**Established design precedents to evaluate, not automatically implement:** hierarchical/structure-aware chunking and table-header retention (Docling); bounded contextual augmentation (document layout parsing); independent extraction followed by separately governed semantic relationship synthesis (GraphRAG-style separation); entity/source/agent identity for annotation and provenance (W3C Web Annotation/PROV). None alone proves semantic completeness or authorizes a source relationship.

## 0.B. Revised reason (v0.2; additional hypothesis, not a retroactive CK change)

The original reason remains valid: not all connected source evidence fits one provider request, and table/visual relations cannot acquire authority by proximity or LLM inference. But the initial proposal imposed an unnecessarily binary outcome: **either the whole document can be faithfully interpreted now or useful analysis must wait**.

Atlas is a **fallible knowledge exploration and governance system**. Its productive boundary should be: *offer available reviewable, explicitly provisional source-grounded findings even when some other evidence is ambiguous, too large or not yet interpreted*; do **not** claim full document coverage, semantic correctness or canonical truth. A warning such as "Atlas can make mistakes" is never a substitute for exact coverage and evidence lineage.

This revision tests **two separate properties**:
1. **Structural integrity (strict):** which source was examined, original document and locator identity, what context was authorized, what was deferred/blocked, who owns each unit, request size, and whether anything was fabricated or silently omitted. Invalid scope, spoofed provenance, unverified source content, invented authoritative joins or failed integrity checks must still fail closed.
2. **Epistemic uncertainty / incomplete coverage (tolerated with disclosure):** fallible but reviewable *proposals* may be produced from independent safe units even if another unit is context-blocked; the blocked unit is **not** `non_fact`, not implicitly processed, and cannot contaminate another proposal. Proposals remain corrigible; neither `needs_resolution: false` nor a valid locator proves semantic accuracy.

**Revised experiment question:** Can an isolated deterministic plan and mock-result/coverage simulation prove that independent work continues, missing work is explicitly accounted for, and any downstream review/reconciliation is limited to available candidates, **without** relaxing canonical acceptance, evidence authorization, source accounting, or the present `semantic_ready` gate?

**Why run this before architecture approval:** We need to know whether a small coverage/dependency *side record* plus qualified local structure is sufficient for a useful V1 workflow, whether a trusted table-to-visual relationship contract is truly necessary for a subset, and whether partial-review lifecycle changes must be separately owned. The purpose is not to force a pre-extraction human gate, to preapprove a sidecar, or to label hypothetical model output as production-ready.

## 1. Revised frozen hypotheses, falsifiers, and boundaries

- **H1 — partitioning and parity:** For independently interpretable synthetic text and intact table units, deterministic exact-once source ownership and bounded requests can coexist with unchanged legacy packet/schema/prompt bytes. **Falsify:** changed approved baseline behavior, invented IDs, omitted/duplicate eligible source identity, nondeterministic plan.
- **H2 — bounded dependency handling:** Non-transitive, *explicitly authorized* essential context can be repeated within a finite request budget while original ownership stays unique; an entire connected component is not automatically one indivisible batch. **Falsify:** transitive/proximity-only propagation, context becoming output owner, missing required context, budget overrun.
- **H3 — structural blocker isolation:** An unproven table↔legend link or an irreducible overbudget source blocks only its *dependent planning/extraction unit* in the simulation; independent unaffected units remain plan-eligible and yield mock reviewable proposals. Every deferred unit retains exact source identity, reason and dependency scope. **Falsify:** global stoppage without integrity necessity, silent success/truncation, missing ledger, model-generated patch to missing context, or false complete result.
- **H4 — provisional results remain provisional:** An incomplete document may have distinct, valid *mock provisional fragments* and an auditable coverage ledger; it must **not** pass the existing canonical complete-document acceptance path, acquire `semantic_ready`, or implicitly authorize reconciliation/publication. **Falsify:** missing source classified `non_fact`, partial fragment presented as accepted `atlas.semantic.extract/v1`, or full-completion claim without complete coverage.
- **H5 — fallibility and correction:** Evidence locators and JSON schema can validate format and provenance without proving that a model's interpretation is correct. A known erroneous but locator-valid **mock** assertion remains contestable, and a synthetic reviewer correction preserves prior proposal and source lineage. **Falsify:** test reports model correctness from structural tests, silently rewrites original proposal, promotes correction to Master, or treats `needs_resolution: false` as a guarantee.
- **H6 — reconciliation boundary:** A *purely simulated* comparison of available provisional candidates may show supported, conflicting, or unresolved relationships **conditioned on known coverage gaps**, but it cannot assert absence of a conflict in deferred material or create an authoritative source↔image join. **Falsify:** missing context interpreted as no rule, source relationships inferred from candidate comparison, or actual legacy reconciliation execution on unaccepted partial results.

**Explicit non-goals:** proving real semantic accuracy, human attestation correctness, source grouping completeness for arbitrary PDFs, reliable visual reasoning, real provider admission/resource accounting, lossless segmentation of all tables, real acceptance of partial results, or production reconciliation under incomplete coverage. The existing reconciliation selector's 500-current-candidate/1 MiB context bound is **not** changed.

**Only proposed runnable scope (pending exact separate authorization):** `scripts/semctx-spike-001/` with fixture inputs, gold, checksums, tiny test-only deterministic planner, **separate test-only coverage ledger**, static mock provisional candidate fixtures, mock comparison/correction verifier, tests and README. Reviewable simulated outputs are **not** canonical Semantic V1 envelopes. Do not import simulator code into production runtime or persist simulated outputs to Atlas databases.

**Frozen separation of records (experiment-only; no production schema selected):**
- `source_inventory`: exact original document SHA + page/locator identity; every eligible source appears once;
- `dependency_assertions`: optional/comparison versus essential context, authority flag, scope and evidence identity; proposed or mock-only edges are not Atlas-authored trusted provenance;
- `work_plan`: eligible owned units + bounded context OR an explicit per-unit deferral/block;
- `processing_coverage`: for each source, `processed_mock`, `deferred_uninterpreted`, or `structurally_non_meaningful_mock` only where justified; the latter is **never** a fallback for meaningful content; optional `attempted`, `reason`, and dependency IDs;
- `reviewable_mock_proposals`: static, source-referenced fixture objects, marked untrusted, correction history and limitations, not accepted candidates.

**Never blur:** a skipped provider attempt is not an examined source; a source classified `non_fact` is not equivalent to an unexamined image; a proposal with no identified ambiguity is still not necessarily accurate. All document-level coverage must be derivable from the ledger, not asserted by the model.

**Forbidden:** production file changes, DB writes/migrations, real API/provider work, actual Docling conversion/image reads, persistence of real derived media, real queue/jobs/permits/admission, new canonical classifications or semantic kinds, prompt edits, actual reconciliation/acceptance, automatic Master/publication, untrusted promotion to sidecar authority, changing original historical gold, importing private PRDs.

## 3. Four immutable fixture families and independently frozen gold

**F1 — complete independent case (compatibility):** synthetic `NormalizedDocument V1` with self-contained paragraph and intact table (headers and qualifiers inside table), no essential external dependencies. Gold: complete eligible source ownership, deterministic fit and mock coverage `complete`; original baseline packet hash/behavior unchanged. No model correctness claim.

**F2 — missing external context with independent work:** table T depends on a visual legend V with no trusted relation; independent paragraph P is safe. Include unlabeled `assetRef` V, same-page/overlap/similar wording and fixture-pairing negative cases, foreign/stale/changed legend hash, duplicated/missing locator. Gold: no authorized T↔V association, T (and other affected units) `deferred_uninterpreted`, V must not be silently `non_fact`; P yields an isolated mock provisional result with its own evidence and *partial document coverage*. A separately named **mock-only** positive control tests that a hypothetical vetted T↔V edge enables planning, but authorizes nothing in production.

**F3 — oversized connected component with mixed outcomes:** fixed A–F synthetic nodes/typed directed edges, a connected graph whose total material exceeds the fixture limit, an independent or bounded-context subset that fits, and one mandatory source+context group that does not. Include indivisible large table with merged-header/shared-condition semantics and unsatisfied/cyclic dependencies; no unqualified row splitting. Gold: exact one-owner inventory, stable bounded request plans for fitting units, explicit deferrals for unfit units, no silent context truncation, no falsely complete aggregate or readiness. Mere graph connectivity/optional/comparison edges do not imply mandatory same-batch inclusion.

**F4 — known model-style mistake + constrained reconciliation (entirely mocked):** same source excerpt associated with two **synthetic proposed interpretations**, including one demonstrably overgeneralized rule (e.g., original "Approval may be required for selected requests" versus proposed "Approval is required for all requests"). Both carry valid locator shapes; the simulator must distinguish **structural validation from semantic correctness** and preserve a human-declared correction as an *additional auditable mock review record* without destroying the first version. Add two grounded provisional candidates with a potential contradiction and a deferred third source: a test-only comparison may identify the known relationship but must state `coverage_incomplete`, never `no other conflicts` or a canonical winner. Static gold states which claims are demonstrably unsupported; no LLM evaluator or inference of accuracy from hashes.

**Gold freeze before implementation:** exact fixture SHA-256 manifest, original locator/source ownership and dispositions, authorized versus mock-only versus rejected edges, expected dependency closure and fit/unfit sets, complete versus partial coverage accounting, expected correction/evidence lineage, expected failure reasons, and legacy baseline hashes. Gold must be reviewed independently from the simulator and not altered to make it pass. Do not fabricate historical or user PRD fixtures.

## 4. Budget, binary oracles, and expected classifications

- **Synthetic limit only:** each *complete serialized simulated request* must be at most **4096 UTF-8 bytes**, including owned slots, repeated context, IDs, role/scope metadata. Freeze finite maximum slot/context counts in the spike test profile. This is a **forced test bound**, not real provider token capacity, image-token pricing or `RequestResourceEnvelope`.
- **O-01 — old behavior unchanged:** no modifications to legacy builder, Zod schema, default `prompt-003/v1` and hashes; baseline suite runs; any original mismatch is declared, not patched by refreshing gold.
- **O-02 — exact inventory:** every eligible original source is exactly once in a work owner or explicitly deferred; a context reference never becomes another owner; no absent image masquerades as `non_fact`; source coverage union is complete and no source appears in incompatible states.
- **O-03 — authorized dependencies:** essential edges are non-transitive unless explicitly and independently justified; no default proximity/fixture/model inference; mock-vetted edges stay mock-only; stale/cross-scope/substituted evidence rejected.
- **O-04 — bounded and deterministic plans:** full serialized payload `<=4096` bytes, zero silent truncation; ten identical runs and source-input order perturbation produce identical canonical plan, source identities, hashes and coverage decisions, except ordering explicitly meaningful in the input.
- **O-05 — useful partial progress:** if F2/F3 have blocked units, unaffected units still produce **mock** reviewable fragments with exact original evidence, while every blocked unit has a distinct reason and original dependency identity; document aggregate remains `incomplete` and `semantic_ready` is never emitted.
- **O-06 — hard integrity failure:** invalid document scope, forged locator, missing verified source, hash substitution or fabricated trusted edge **rejects the affected authority-dependent operation**; an untrusted mismatch may not be downgraded to an ordinary semantic caveat or treated as valid provisional evidence.
- **O-07 — fallibility/corrigibility:** mock known-wrong assertion is not accepted as truth because it happens to have valid refs and `needs_resolution: false`; reviewer correction retains original and corrected meaning/evidence with distinct provenance, never updates Master.
- **O-08 — reconciliation honesty:** mock comparisons are conditional on available candidates and explicitly report uncovered regions; no source-link establishment, cross-context inference, negative proof of absent conflict, or live reconciliation stage/job.
- **O-09 — isolation:** static import review plus instrumented forbidden-call counters demonstrate zero provider calls, image reads, jobs, DB writes, admission/reservations and production mutations.

**Outcome vocabulary is experiment-only:** `FULL_STRUCTURAL_PLAN`, `PARTIAL_REVIEWABLE_PLAN`, `SOURCE_RELATIONSHIP_UNPROVEN`, `IRREDUCIBLE_CONTEXT_OVERFLOW`, `INTEGRITY_REJECTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`. Multiple outcomes may apply to one document: e.g., partial planning succeeds while a specific source remains blocked. A correct overflow/deferred or integrity-rejection observation **can satisfy its negative oracle**; no forced all-green semantic outcome.

**Acceptance criterion for the spike report:** all O-01–O-09 demonstrated against immutable fixtures with exact observed evidence; F1 complete, F2/F3 *both* safe provisional progress and honest defer, F4 correction/limited comparison. This proves only feasibility of **structural simulated** partial progress, not provider quality, semantic accuracy or production acceptance.

## 6. Explicit stop budget / non-negotiable integrity

- **Budget:** one isolated simulator pass; maximum **one** targeted correction for a reproduced implementation defect against **unchanged independently frozen gold**. If unchanged root failure, missing trusted issuer/oracle, need for production lifecycle acceptance changes, or pressure to weaken gold, STOP as `PLANNING_DECISION_REQUIRED`. No third implementation variant, unbounded heuristics or vendor/model switching.
- **Progress allowed:** unproven semantic relationship, meaningful yet uninterpreted visual, bounded overflow, undecidable ambiguity, and a known-wrong synthetic interpretation may coexist with **unaffected mock reviewable proposals**. They do *not* authorize the affected source's assertion.
- **Hard rejects:** invented/unverifiable locators, wrong project/document scope, corrupted hash, forged trusted relationship, incorrect owned-source accounting, or unauthorized proof of semantic readiness/truth. None becomes "Atlas can make mistakes" warning.
- **Return `INCONCLUSIVE` honestly** if independent source semantics or partitionability cannot be established by the frozen fixtures. This outcome itself documents where a later provider or human qualification would be required.
