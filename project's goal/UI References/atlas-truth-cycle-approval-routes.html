Safara operating model

3 source PRDs · base Truth v5

Atlas currently reads the project as one connected journey: prepare a departure, register a pilgrim, complete requirements, confirm readiness, then finalize a stable manifest.

01
Departure and registration

Registration requires an open departure with remaining capacity. A pilgrim cannot have two active registrations for the same departure, and the registration price becomes the agreed price.

PRD 1 · pp. 2–3
02
Payment verification

Only payments marked Received reduce the remaining balance. Rejected payments stay visible in the history and do not change the balance.

PRD 2 · p. 2
03
Readiness is a gated decision

A pilgrim is ready only when remaining payment is zero, required documents are Valid, the passport is available and valid, and the visa is Approved.

PRD 2 · p. 4
04
Blockers and final manifest

If a condition is unmet, readiness must show the active blocker. Only ready pilgrims enter the final manifest, which remains a stable snapshot after finalization.
